# Curated handover — Lantern

*Template. Goes to `docs/guild/HANDOVER.md` in the adopting repository.
Every role profile lists it first in its mandatory reading, and `guild-hi`
reports it beside the latest journal entry. It is the digest; the journal
(`docs/guild/journal/`) is the record. When the journal is newer, entry
shows both under a warning and the journal wins on anything recent.*

Revision: `<date>` · written by `<seat>` · at `<branch> @ <short sha>`

Rewrite it whole when a revision is due; never append to it. A revision is
due when a priority, a ruling or a seat changes, and it carries the date
each entry below stops being true.

## Current priority

One sentence, and the date or the event after which it must be decided
again. A priority with no expiry becomes a stale one that a fresh session
takes for current.

- `<priority>` · valid until `<date or event>`

## Open decisions and who owns them

- `<decision>` · owner `<role>` · blocking `<issue or none>`

## Rulings in force

Only the rulings a new session would otherwise re-derive, each with where
it was taken.

- `<ruling>` · `<link to the comment or issue>` · valid until `<date or event>`

## Seats, as they run today

Read from each seat's charter, never from this file's history. One line per
seat: where it runs, and what keeps it inside its role.

- Chief Architect · `<harness and host>` · `<the mechanism, e.g. session markers stripped from every mission>`

## What a new session must not do

The traps the last sessions paid for, one line each, with the register
entry that paid for it.

- `<trap>` · `<register entry>`

## Next smallest action

- `<action>` · `<owner>`
