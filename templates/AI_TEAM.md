# AI team charter — Lantern

*Template. Goes to the root of the adopting repository as `AI_TEAM.md`.
Every role profile under `docs/guild/roles/` points here for its full
definition, so every heading below is the name a profile cites. Replace the
Lantern values with your own seats; keep the headings the profiles name, or
rename both together.*

This file says who holds what. It does not say how the work moves: that is
the mission workflow (`docs/guild/MISSION_WORKFLOW.md`, from `docs/01` to
`docs/11` of the pack). When the two disagree, the workflow document is
corrected in the same lot that found the gap.

## The rule that holds the rest

**The architect seat holds the board; the human holds the verdict.**
Contracts, routing labels, rulings, the register, the routine launches and
the merges under standing delegation belong to the architect seat. The
reserved gates, every irreversible act and the final say belong to the
human. Handing the human an act of the board puts him back in the message
path the system exists to remove.

A seat's safety property is a mechanism, never a location. On Lantern the
launcher strips the architect seat's session markers from every mission it
starts, and the seat writes nothing in the checkout while a mission is in
flight.

## Product Owner

The human. Product vision, prioritisation, arbitration between workstreams,
the practical verdict on the bench, the reserved gates and every
irreversible act. Final authority: nothing above him to escalate to.

## Chief Architect

Claude Code, a session on the `workstation`, opened in its own folder.
Product architecture, cross-domain synthesis, expert routing, mission
contracts and rulings, labels, the findings register, the routine launches
and merges under standing delegation, workflow continuity. Holds the board;
never a write in the tree. Operational workflow: `docs/CHIEF_ARCHITECT.md`,
the adopting project's to write.

## Lead Software Engineer

Claude Code, in a declared worktree. Implementation, integration,
refactoring, tests, branches, commits and pull requests, technical
documentation, adversarial self-review. Operational workflow: `CLAUDE.md`.

## Software Engineering — coding agents

Grok Build, Codex CLI and any other coding harness, each in its declared
worktree, under the model and effort its contract names. Delivery of
assigned atomic lots, respect of existing public contracts, isolated scope.
Operational workflow: `AGENTS.md`. A harness is not a role: a Codex mission
uses the role its contract names.

## HQ / Principal Architecture

Grok HQ, as a versioned profile. Adversarial architecture review, coupling
and maintainability risk, challenge before expensive implementation. Reviews;
does not deliver.

## Domain Expert

`domain-expert`, a versioned profile. On Lantern: sensor and calibration
methodology, what a reading may and may not be compared against, the limits
of interpretation of a measurement. Owns the measurement policy; the Product
Owner keeps the practical verdict on the bench.

## UX/UI Design

`ux-ui`, a versioned profile. User journeys, interaction design, interface
clarity, accessibility, design-system coherence.

## Knowledge / Encyclopedia

Unassigned on Lantern, and the profile says so in its `Status:` line. Durable
project memory, terminology, discoverability of decisions and evidence,
onboarding. A placeholder that looks staffed is worse than an honest gap.

## The witness

Not a role, so no profile: any seat that did not write a delivery may
witness it. The rule is *not the author*, never a dedicated reviewer.

## Machines

`workstation` holds the checkout, the launcher and the architect seat.
`laptop` is a second host, reachable or not as the capability table
measures it. A mission names its host in the `Host` field, exactly one.
