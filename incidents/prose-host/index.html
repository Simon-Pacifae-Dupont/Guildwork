The prose host

What broke

A mission's criteria named the laptop in prose; every field in the contract matched the workstation perfectly, and it ran there. The seat then measured the laptop unreachable from the workstation. The same morning another mission's harness turned out not to be authenticated on the host it was launched on. No check anywhere could have seen either.

The rule it paid for

The Host field, a closed vocabulary of machine names, exactly one.
